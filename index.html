Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

StickyCart ("the App", "we", "us") is a third-party application available on the Wix App Market, developed by Michael Adam (Brivano Labs). Site owners ("Site Owners") install the App on their online store to show a sticky Add to Cart bar to their visitors ("Shoppers").

2. Data We Collect

StickyCart does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The App has no servers and no database of its own.

  • The bar is a small script that runs only in the Shopper's browser, on the store's product pages.
  • It reads the product options shown on the page (for example size or color) only to check that the Shopper has chosen them, and then uses the store's own Add to Cart button.
  • Products, prices, stock, carts and checkout are handled entirely by the store platform, under the store's and Wix's own policies. StickyCart never sees or receives cart contents, order details or payment information.
  • As part of installing any app, Wix may make basic site details (such as the site ID) available to the App. We do not store or use them.

3. How We Use Data

Because we do not collect data, we do not use, sell, rent or share any data, and we do not use data for advertising, analytics or profiling.

4. Third Parties

StickyCart does not send data to any third party and uses no sub-processors. The App's script is delivered by Wix as part of the store's site.

5. Cookies & Tracking

StickyCart does not set cookies, does not use browser storage, and does not use tracking pixels or analytics scripts.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you still have the right to ask us about your data; contact us at the email address below and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

StickyCart is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

8. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App starts handling any data, we will update this policy before that change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
